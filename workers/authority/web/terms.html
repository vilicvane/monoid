The service is optional

Monoid Authority issues authority bundles to Monoid Java Card wallet cards and signs time proofs used for card OTP. Bundle owners can be Google accounts, GitHub accounts or organizations, or Ethereum wallets. You choose whether to use this authority from the Monoid app.

Understand whom you trust

The operator can derive bundle keys. Trusting an authority therefore means trusting its operator, including its ability to issue the same bundle to another card.

Monoid Authority never holds your card private keys or funds. However, an issued bundle lets cards exchange data with every member of that bundle. Only join a bundle whose owner, members and peer-extension policy you trust. Deleting server records or ending a sign-in does not revoke bundles already on cards.
